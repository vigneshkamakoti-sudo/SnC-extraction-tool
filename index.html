3Normalise ME consumption (optional)

Brings every row to the same displacement and the ordered speed: ME/24h × (normalisation displacement ÷ row displacement)exp × (ordered speed ÷ SOW)exp. Each vessel needs a small draft–displacement table (two points, ballast and laden, are enough; more points are more accurate) so every row's draft can be turned into a displacement, plus the normalisation draft and displacement for ballast and for laden.

4Review consumption

Three separate graphs: ME consumption (MT/day), SOW against ordered speed, and mean draft. Every row that passed your filters is plotted against the benchmark average for its ordered speed.

Chart view
Coloured dots = daily rows (blue ballast, orange laden). Each horizontal benchmark line is the average for one ordered speed and is coloured to match that speed's shade in the tables. Click a point to jump to its row · Shift+click to exclude / re-include · grey ✕ = rows you excluded.

5Select rows

Tick = included in the averages and the Excel output. Untick any row to exclude it. You can also tick a row that the filters rejected to force it in (only if it has a valid duration and ordered speed).
